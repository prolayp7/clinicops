## Exclusions (§7)

- Multi-tenant SaaS and independent clinic databases.
- Native iOS or Android apps.
- Insurance claims, eligibility, ICD/CPT automation or clearinghouses.
- Certified e-prescribing, EPCS, pharmacy networks.
- HL7/FHIR exchange with external systems.
- Laboratory instruments or external laboratory APIs.
- Telehealth/video calling.
- Data migration from an existing system.
- AI diagnosis or treatment recommendations.
- Formal HIPAA certification, legal advice, penetration testing and organizational compliance documentation.
- Complex analytics builder, accounting ledger and inventory management.

These remain excluded, rather than release defects. Missing Phase-1 functionality above must not be relabeled Phase 2.
